How to read this experiment

All effects use condition − intact. Positive loss changes mean harm. Negative probability and accuracy changes mean harm. Interaction signs are metric-dependent: a negative accuracy interaction indicates more joint damage than the sum of the individual changes.

Paired bootstrap samples preserve the four balanced answer-position strata. The 10,000 resamples use the same examples for all conditions. Family intervals use the centered standardized maximum over all 28 pairs × 3 metrics within one seed. They are approximate, not an exact coverage guarantee. Pointwise effect intervals are unadjusted. No interval combines training seeds.
